# DocuSense

Ask questions of research papers and get answers with verified citations.

Most RAG systems treat a PDF as a flat wall of text. DocuSense parses the
structure of a paper — title, authors, venue, year, sections, citations — and
uses it at query time. Every passage an answer quotes comes back with the
section it came from and a citation checked against the source.

**Try it without signing up:** sign in as `demo@docusense.app` with the password
`read-the-papers`. Two papers are already on the shelf, and they disagree with
each other, so "how do these two compare?" is a fair question to ask.

You can also register your own account and upload a paper. Documents are visible
only to the account that uploaded them.

## What is measured

Retrieval is benchmarked on [QASPER](https://allenai.org/data/qasper) — 80
papers, 1,048 chunks, 259 questions with evidence grounded to real chunk ids:

| Retrieval | MRR | NDCG@10 | P@1 | Recall@10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vector only | 0.1867 | 0.1949 | 0.1004 | 0.2938 |
| + BM25, fused with RRF | 0.2244 | 0.2348 | 0.1313 | 0.3438 |
| **+ cross-encoder rerank** | **0.2824** | **0.2771** | **0.2046** | **0.3816** |

Hybrid search is worth +20.2% MRR (p = 0.0003); reranking a further +25.8%
(p = 0.0044). Section routing was measured, found to cost accuracy, and turned
off — the reasoning is written up rather than the feature quietly removed.

## Notes on this instance

- Answers are generated by a hosted model, not the local `llama3.2:3b` the
  project uses on a workstation. A free CPU Space cannot run a 3B model at a
  speed anyone would wait for.
- Storage is ephemeral. A restart clears accounts and uploads, and the demo
  shelf reseeds itself.
- Accounts are capped on documents and upload size, because this is a public URL.
