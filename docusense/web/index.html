§ 01 — The difference

The same question, asked two ways

Generic retrieval chunks a PDF and hopes the right paragraph floats up. It usually gets close. Close is not a citation.

Flat retrieval

BERT achieved around 93–94% on SST-2.
  • No section awareness — the number could be from related work
  • No source you can check
  • Nothing to paste into a literature review

§ 02 — What it knows

Structure, not just text

01

It finds the paper inside the PDF

Title, authors, venue, year, DOI, abstract, twenty section types, and citations in both numbered and author–year form — with a confidence score, so a lecture handout is not treated as a journal article.

02

It sends the question to the right section

“How did they train it?” searches methodology. “What accuracy?” searches results. “Why is this better?” searches discussion. The routing is pattern-based, so it works whether or not an LLM is reachable.

03

It reads your filters out of plain English

“Papers from 2020–2023 by Bengio” becomes a year range and an author filter applied inside the vector search, before ranking — not bolted on afterwards where it would quietly break the top results.

04

It keeps exact terms exact

Embeddings think BERT-base and RoBERTa are neighbours. BM25 knows they are not. Both rankings are fused, so meaning and precision survive together.

05

It deletes citations it cannot prove

Small models invent plausible references even when told not to. Every citation is checked against the retrieved sources by author and year. Unsupported ones are removed rather than shown to you.

§ 03 — Method

Three steps, no configuration

  1. 1

    Drop in a PDF

    It is converted, cleaned, parsed for structure, split along section boundaries, embedded, and indexed. A 30-page survey takes about half a minute.

  2. 2

    Ask in your own words

    Your phrasing decides where it looks and what it filters on. Follow-up questions inherit the thread, so “what about its accuracy?” resolves against whatever you were just discussing.

  3. 3

    Read the answer with its receipts

    Text arrives as it is written, each claim tied to the chunk it came from, with an APA reference list and BibTeX export underneath.
